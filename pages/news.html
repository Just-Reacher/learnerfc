Former University of Ghana SRC President, H.E. Prince Asumadu, Supports Learners FC

H.E. Prince Asumadu, former SRC President of the University of Ghana, Legon, has generously contributed 50% of the cost of new goalpost nets for the club's training ground at Malejor.

Learners Football Club is delighted to announce the kind support of H.E. Prince Asumadu, former President of the Students' Representative Council (SRC) of the University of Ghana, Legon.

Hon. Prince Asumadu has generously contributed 50% of the cost of new football goalpost nets for the club's training ground at Malejor, a timely intervention for a club that has been training and playing competitive matches without proper nets due to financial constraints.

Club NewsAugust 21, 2026

Coach's Philosophy

A first look at the thinking behind Learn, Relearn, Unlearn, and how it shapes daily life at the club.

"Learn" means mastering the fundamentals properly the first time. "Relearn" means revisiting those fundamentals under pressure, in matches, when the easy version of a skill breaks down. "Unlearn" is the hardest part — letting go of shortcuts and bad habits picked up before joining the club.

That three-part philosophy now sits behind every training plan, every substitution, and every post-match conversation at Learners FC.
