The team guiding Learners FC's players day to day. Roles are shown as they are confirmed.

Regan Azalekor

Assistant Coach

Regan's coaching identity centers on controlling the game by controlling the ball — compact, possession-based football combined with quick transitions and a high press. Regan's coaching identity centers on controlling the game by controlling the ball — compact, possession-based football combined with quick transitions and a high press. He works within a 3-5-2 system built for both defensive security and attacking flexibility. In possession, his teams build from the back and create numerical superiority in midfield; out of possession, they press high and transition instantly. My teams are compact, disciplined and relentless out of possession — winning the ball high and transitioning instantly. His approach draws on the influence of Pep Guardiola (his biggest influence, for a winning mentality and mastery of positional play), Jürgen Klopp (for high-pressing and transition play), and Antonio Conte (for proving the 3-5-2 can be both compact and devastating). At Learners FC, we don't just play football — we learn to understand it.
